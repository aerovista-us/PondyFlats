Two homes · one lot · Pennsylvania access only

Four design-development paths for Lot 2.

This is a concept study, not a permit set. The four options use the same surveyed parcel and keep modeled vehicle access on Pennsylvania. Each option has different tradeoffs in garage placement, maneuvering, home geometry and unresolved professional/AHJ validation.

If you’re new here

  1. The lot does not rotate.

    Pennsylvania is the right/front edge of every drawing; the same survey geometry is carried through every option.

  2. Parking is the hard problem.

    A full-size SUV/pickup is the common design vehicle. Each concept states exactly what part of entry, parking and exit has actually been demonstrated.

  3. There are four live concept paths.

    Designs 1–3 remain intact. Design 4 adds two full 22×22 detached rear garages and a new north-finger home arrangement.

  4. Zoning/code evidence stays conditional until confirmed.

    Concept geometry and published-code research are not substitutes for parcel-specific zoning, fire/building, civil, survey or permit review.
