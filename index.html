Two defensible ways to build on Lot 2.

This is a concept study, not a permit set. Both designs keep vehicle access on Pennsylvania, use a full-size SUV as the test, and sit on the surveyed 7,028 SF parcel. Pick a package below. To try a different parcel, use Your lot — that page does not redesign Lot 2.

If you’re new here

Lot 2 is narrow and irregular. Pretty pictures are not allowed to invent a different building. Read in this order:

  1. The lot does not rotate.

    Pennsylvania is the right / front edge of every drawing. The compass points left on purpose.

  2. Parking is the hard problem.

    A full-size SUV / pickup must enter from Pennsylvania, reach each garage independently, and leave again — on Lot 2, not through a neighbor.

  3. There are two live concepts.

    Design 1 keeps garages in the house plates (two enclosed + two covered). Design 2 puts two detached 20×20 garages in the rear yard as accessory structures.

  4. Zoning is still a hypothesis until the city says yes.

    Design 2’s 5′ rear garage setback comes from the city’s lots-without-alley sheet. It is not a permit.
