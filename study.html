Pass 1.5 workflow

The seven original concepts are not fully validated under the old rectangular envelope. Pass 1.5 re-seats every footprint inside the polygon-accurate survey and working setback envelope before any Pass 2 architecture spend.

SOT survey polygon (frozen) → polygon-accurate setbacks (planning assumptions) → concept geometry (12 strategies) → validation metrics (PASS / REVIEW / FAIL) → approve shortlist (3 finalists) → Pass 2 site / elevation (one finalist at a time)
Pass 1.5 validates geometry only. Swept-path, fire separation, utilities, ownership structure, final zoning/setbacks, and engineering remain unverified.
