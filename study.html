3. Orientation correction

What was wrong

Pennsylvania = east/right

Generated diagrams and the old SVG placed Pennsylvania on the right with a 148′ east–west depth. Survey bearings contradict this.

What governs now

Boundary-table bearings

N 0°22′36″ E on 148′ = south–north. S 89°37′24″ E on 50′ = west–east. Decorative labels on the master image are ignored.
