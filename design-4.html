What remains conditional

The four-stall static fit is modeled, but Design 4 is not released until the autonomous path audit passes and its evidence is promoted into the package. Parcel-specific accessory zoning also still requires confirmation. Keeping both 22×22 garages on the same far-rear line leaves about 2 ft clear between the structures; building/fire/eave/drainage/AHJ requirements for that condition remain unresolved.

Professional-validation boundary

This package supports concept selection and design development. Zoning, building/fire code, structural, MEP, civil, drainage, survey/easement, energy, and permit validation remain professional/AHJ work. Nothing here is labeled permit-ready, code-approved, zoning-approved, structurally validated, civil-certified, or construction-ready.
