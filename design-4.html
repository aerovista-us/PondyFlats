Two full 22×22 detached garages at the rear

Design 4 starts from a different rule than Design 3: preserve two genuine full-size detached two-car garages, push them into the rear accessory zone, and make the homes and driveway solve around them. The promoted north-finger seed is not frozen yet and is not a permit set.

Vehicle/garage result

The locked 20.5 × 8.0 ft full-size SUV/pickup can reach both garages with the current 25 ft minimum rear-axle turning assumption and can fit fully inside each 22×22 plate. Back-out is represented as a reverse-equivalent traversal of the same collision-free pose envelope. Design 4 does not claim that the vehicle turns around inside the garage or exits forward.

What remains conditional

The current Coeur d'Alene published code supports the normal 5 ft rear / 5 ft side accessory planning baseline, but parcel-specific zoning confirmation is still required. With both 22×22 garages on the same far-rear line, only about 2 ft remains between the garage plates. That garage-to-garage spacing still needs building/fire/eave/drainage/AHJ confirmation. If more spacing is required, Design 4 will stagger a garage forward while retaining both full 22×22 plates.
