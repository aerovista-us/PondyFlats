Elevations

Customer-facing concept elevations on the frozen CFB-716 footprints. The architectural direction is restrained North Idaho contemporary: warm horizontal siding, grounded stone/masonry accents, dark roof planes, simple wood entries, and larger private-side glazing.

Presentation intent: readable as a real home, while still clearly labeled concept-stage. The exterior/site geometry remains frozen.
A prettier façade may not move a frozen CFB-716 core volume. Roof articulation, openings, trim, and materials may develop inside that rule.
