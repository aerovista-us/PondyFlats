What the newer LotScope automation adds to the next iteration

The next Design 4 repair pass does not need to defend one fixed driveway sketch. LotScope can compare several legal correction strategies while keeping the hard vehicle, parcel, setback, door, collision, and parking gates intact.

Move or re-proportion buildings

Residential mass can shift or change proportions inside its legal/program envelope when that creates a cleaner circulation corridor.

Move or resize garages

Garage position and credible plate size can trade against house mass and access geometry instead of being treated as immovable.

Reshape the driveway

Local control points, approach geometry, and maneuver paths remain available repair tools for the outbound and tight-door cases.

Add pavement when it actually helps

Localized apron or corridor expansion remains a first-class option. Pavement sensitivity tests whether widening solves the hard failure before spending more site area.

Experimental platform capability: detached-garage rotation can now be represented by oriented polygons, doors, and parked poses in LotScope. Rotation is not part of the proven Design 4 evidence shown here and must stay labeled experimental until a dedicated Design 4 audit passes.
