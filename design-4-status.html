What changed in the LotScope automation layer

Design 4 can now be refined inside a broader site-repair toolbox instead of forcing one type of correction. The automation layer compares multiple legal ways to improve a candidate and keeps the evidence gates authoritative.

Move or re-proportion buildings

Residential mass can shift or change proportions inside its legal/program envelope when that produces a cleaner circulation solution.

Move or resize garages

Garage position and credible garage dimensions can trade against house mass and access geometry instead of being treated as fixed obstacles.

Reshape the driveway

Local control points, approach geometry, and maneuver paths remain available repair tools.

Add pavement when it actually helps

Localized apron or corridor expansion is a first-class option. Pavement sensitivity tests whether widening solves the hard failure before spending more site area.

Also in the platform foundation: detached-garage rotation can now be represented by true oriented polygons, doors, and parked poses, but rotation is still experimental and is not claimed as proven Design 4 evidence. General LotScope benchmarking on the current platform retains 40 physical passes, 40 combined passes, and five distinct frozen/promotion-ready site concepts. Those general finalists are separate from this Design 4 four-stall proof.
