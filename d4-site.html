What Workbench now rejects

A centerline that merely misses an obstruction is no longer enough. The gate evaluates the full swept body, minimum fixed-obstruction clearance, survey containment, garage-wall and door crossing, simultaneous parked fit, gear changes, and final enclosed pose. A technically collision-free route with only a few inches of margin is rejected instead of being presented as a customer-facing PASS.
