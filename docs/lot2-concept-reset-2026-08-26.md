# Lot 2 — Concept Reset Brief

**Date:** 2026-08-26

## Why this reset exists

Recent review exposed two problems in the previous design cycle:

1. several concept drawings simplified or misstated the true lot geometry;
2. several layouts spent too much of the best central land on driveway/motor-court pavement while placing garages where they were awkward or impossible to reach.

The new working strategy is to use constrained side-yard land for access first, then preserve the largest possible contiguous area for the two homes.

## New baseline strategy

### Driveway

- All access originates at Pennsylvania.
- First test a shared driveway spine running primarily within the 10 ft side-building-setback corridor.
- Treat driveway pavement and building setbacks as separate planning constraints unless controlling code/engineering review says otherwise.
- Widen, flare, or branch only where actual swept-path geometry requires it.
- Avoid a large central motor court unless the vehicle model proves it is necessary.

### Garages

- Garage doors must face a real reachable driveway/apron.
- Prefer garage placement that intercepts the access route early and reduces pavement.
- Front/Pennsylvania-side garage placement should be tested before automatically pushing garages to the rear.
- Rear garages remain valid when they produce a better proven geometry.
- Detached rear garages may be tested separately under the rear-25 accessory-structure zoning branch.

### Houses

- Do not place or polish houses before driveway and garage access are proven.
- Give the best remaining contiguous envelope to the homes.
- Preserve the target of two comparable homes near ~1,800 SF each where feasible.
- Building masses may move, rotate, lengthen, narrow, or otherwise reset when doing so materially improves the site.

## Historical concepts are reopened

E2, G1, V2, and other promising historical families are **not discarded**.

The old exact coordinates may have failed, but those failures were tied to older driveway, garage, building-placement, and setback assumptions.

Each historical family may now be re-tested as a clean reset variant:

- `E2-R1`, `E2-R2`, ...
- `G1-R1`, `G1-R2`, ...
- `V2-R1`, `V2-R2`, ...

The original source stays untouched.

## Reset workflow

For every reset concept:

1. load the true survey polygon;
2. apply the declared setback/zoning scenario;
3. place the preferred 10 ft side-setback driveway spine;
4. place two garage capacities where they require the least additional pavement;
5. orient each garage door toward a usable approach;
6. add only the pavement required for actual turning and staging;
7. run FS-SUV inbound and outbound swept paths for each garage;
8. score total pavement burden and remaining contiguous home envelope;
9. place/refit the two building masses into the surviving envelope;
10. reject only after the reset geometry, not the old coordinates, fails.

## Immediate discovery set

The next workbench pass should include both new and revived families.

Recommended first batch:

1. **SIDE-SPINE-1** — straight 10 ft setback driveway with two front-biased garages.
2. **SIDE-SPINE-2** — same spine with staggered garage intercepts and minimal local flares.
3. **E2-R1** — rebuild E2 around the side-setback driveway.
4. **G1-R1** — rebuild G1 independently of the existing G1-A micro-fix path.
5. **V2-R1** — rebuild the V/butterfly idea with reachable front-facing garages and true lot geometry.
6. **REAR25-R1** — zoning-dependent detached rear-garage experiment using the rear-25 accessory-structure rule set.

## Scoring priorities

Rank survivors in this order:

1. legal/declared zoning scenario compliance;
2. actual garage accessibility;
3. FS-SUV inbound/outbound clearance;
4. minimum pavement burden;
5. amount and quality of contiguous home-building envelope;
6. ability to produce two comparable homes;
7. architectural opportunity.

## Important caution

The working assumption that open driveway pavement may occupy building-setback land is being used for concept planning. It is not a permit conclusion. Final candidates must still be checked against City driveway-approach rules, drainage, utilities, snow storage, fire/access requirements, easements, and the controlling zoning interpretation.

## Governing idea

**Do not spend the center of the lot on circulation if circulation can live in setback land. Reset old concepts around that rule before declaring them dead.**
