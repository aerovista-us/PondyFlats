# LotScope Workbench audit contract

## Purpose

Workbench is the primary design-development evaluator. External review can discover missing cases, but a discovered defect is converted into a permanent Workbench gate rather than becoming a one-off manual fix.

## Required promotion gates

A concept cannot be promoted as a Workbench PASS unless all applicable gates below succeed on the same exact source head.

1. **Source-of-truth geometry** — parcel, buildings, garages, setbacks and ownership references come from one explicit geometry model.
2. **Vehicle-body truth** — circulation uses the full vehicle body from an axle pose, not centerline clearance alone.
3. **Continuous-enough motion validation** — every motion primitive is sampled between planner states; endpoint-only or coarse chord checks are insufficient.
4. **Fixed-obstruction clearance** — Workbench reports minimum parcel-boundary and fixed-obstacle distance and applies a declared threshold.
5. **Garage crossing** — the swept body must pass through the declared garage opening without wall clipping.
6. **Final enclosed pose** — the entire vehicle body must be inside the garage at the terminal parking pose.
7. **Multi-stall reality** — when more than one stall is claimed, simultaneous parked fit and independent approach with the companion vehicle parked must be tested.
8. **Usable pavement envelope** — site sheets show actual concept lane/apron area and width, not a decorative centerline stroke.
9. **Room circulation** — planning diagrams require physical room adjacency and graph connectivity from entry/stair to required rooms.
10. **Exterior-face truth** — elevations derive from actual polygon boundary segments, including notched/L-shaped shells.
11. **Opening contract** — doors and windows use shared world coordinates plus machine-auditable type attributes and consistent visual coding.
12. **Export parity** — normal repository export reproduces the current concept package; generated standalone packages are tested, not trusted by assumption.
13. **Regression protection** — previously released designs run their existing gates unchanged.
14. **Rendered-pixel QA** — source and standalone pages render without runtime errors across desktop, tablet and mobile viewports before release.

## Repair-before-close behavior

A failed gate does not automatically reduce the customer program. Workbench first searches the permitted repair space: shift/widen circulation, change local pavement, change door geometry, adjust non-locked room partitions or explore another allowed topology. Locked program requirements are reduced only by an explicit product/design decision.

## Status language

`PASS` means the stated Workbench design-development gate passed. `CONDITIONAL` means the geometry may be viable but depends on zoning/AHJ/professional confirmation. `FAIL` means the current model does not meet the gate. Workbench PASS never means zoning approval, code approval, structural validation, civil certification, permit readiness or construction readiness unless a separately authorized professional workflow provides that evidence.

## Design 4 proving case

Pondy Flats Design 4 is the current proving case. The locked program is two detached 22×22 garages and four practical enclosed full-size-vehicle stalls. The exact-stall autonomous audit is stored in `docs/lot2-design-4-workbench-evidence.json` and is regenerated by `scripts/d4-workbench-search-v2.js` in CI.
