## Objective

Advance **J1-B** into a visual ownership/design gate by asking:

> If the site did not force this architecture on us, would we still choose to build it?

The bar is intentionally strict:
- It must initially read as **house + house**.
- Only secondarily may the viewer notice **four enclosed parking spaces**.

## Design requirements encoded in the drawing intent

This page draws the Pass 2C sketch with these specific goals:

1. **Garage-forward reality stays real** (locations unchanged), but is **architecturally subordinate**:
   - garage doors are drawn with **recessed door cues** (physical recess impression),
   - the upper story dominates in height and roof hierarchy.
2. **Two pedestrian entries are explicit** (porch/door markers).
3. **Staggered upper volumes are deliberate**:
   - upper A = **22'**,
   - upper B = **16'**,
   - the silhouette difference is used for two-home identity.
4. **Windows + composition above/beside garages** are shown as building façades (not plain bands).
5. No J1-C, no photorealism yet.
