Design 2 — how to walk the room through it.

Open the client folder’s index.html on the shared screen. Keep this guide. This is a concept package. Do not call it Design Complete. Do not freeze it in the meeting.

One sentence. Two homes facing Pennsylvania, two detached two-car garages in the rear yard, four enclosed stalls — still a hypothesis with the city, still tight at the south edge.

Beats

0:00 · Open · 30 seconds

What they are looking at

A parallel idea to Design 1. Same lot. Different parking. Connected L-duplex toward the street. Two 20×20 detached garages stacked at the rear.

Say: “This is Design 2. Four enclosed parking spaces. It is a complete concept you can read, not construction drawings.”

0:01 · Lot · 90 seconds

Same convention as Design 1

Pennsylvania right / front. Rear left. Compass left. Cars only from Pennsylvania. Gold dashed line = house setbacks. Green dashed line = accessory garage reading.

Say: “If we need north-up, we print a north arrow — we do not rotate the lot.”

0:03 · Walk · 8 minutes

Sheet order

  1. Site (A-001) — point to the two green 20×20 boxes on the left. Point to the drive coming from the right. Stay until they see four enclosed stalls without a rear-yard variance story… then immediately qualify the 5′.
  2. Bubble then plans (A-103 → A-101 / A-102) — topology first. Rooms are conceptual. Living about 1,792 / 1,944.
  3. Pennsylvania elevation (A-201) — you do not see the garages from the street. Home A only.
  4. Axon (A-401 / A-402) — doors outlined red, windows blue. ENTRY A on Pennsylvania. ENTRY B on the south court. Both 16′ garage doors face east toward the drive.

If they only remember one picture, make it the site, then the axon.

0:11 · Honest · 3 minutes

What you will not promise

  • 5′ accessory rear is a reading of the city’s lots-without-alley sheet. Coeur d’Alene has not confirmed it for this lot.
  • SUV clearance at the south edge is about 1½ feet — workable, not generous. Call it a concept package, not a frozen pass.
  • Home A is 26 feet deep because 28 feet pinched the swept path. Do not reopen 28 in the meeting.
  • Do not mix these plates with Design 1.
  • ENTRY A on the plan vs the axon is still a known gap. Do not freeze until they agree. Do not hide it if someone notices.
0:14 · Questions

Likely pushes

  • “Is the 5′ legal?” — Hypothesis. If the city says no, this concept is in trouble; we do not invent alley access to save it.
  • “Can we keep Design 1’s living and this parking?” — That is a new brief, not a mix of the two drawings.
  • “The south side looks tight.” — Yes. 1.54 feet. We are not calling it generous.
  • “When is it frozen?” — Only if you explicitly ask. Today it is a concept package.

Talking points (print this)

  • Same Lot 2 survey as Design 1. Different brief: four enclosed, accessory rear stack.
  • Garages: west face x=5, 20×20, doors east, 6′ north buffer (not the 3′ exception).
  • Homes: duplex west 52 / party 92. Home A 36×26. Drive spine y=37.
