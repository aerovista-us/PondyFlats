Massing truth and architectural axon

A-401 and A-402 deliberately use the same projection. The clean architectural axon adds openings from the same world-coordinate source used by the elevations rather than hand-placing doors or windows. Roof surfaces are deliberately suppressed until validated Workbench ridge/pitch geometry can be projected exactly from the shared roof SOT; the dashed top outline is only the working wall/plate datum.

What stays synchronized

Site wall masses come from the Design 4 geometry model. Exterior entries come from the plan-closure model. Garage doors and concept windows live in one shared world opening array. Roof status comes from the shared roof SOT and currently requires suppression. QA checks the same camera, opening metadata, and roof render policy.
