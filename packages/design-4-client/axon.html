Same camera · same world geometry

Massing truth and architectural axon

A-401 and A-402 deliberately use the same projection. Openings and roof surfaces come from the same world-coordinate sources used by the elevations. The roof faces are the validated 3D surfaces exported by LotScope; ridges and valleys remain tied to that solved geometry rather than being redrawn for presentation.

What stays synchronized

Site wall masses come from the Design 4 geometry model. Exterior entries come from the plan-closure model. Garage doors and concept windows live in one shared world opening array. Exact roof surfaces come from the shared LotScope roof SOT. QA checks the same camera, opening metadata, surface-face authority and roof render policy.
