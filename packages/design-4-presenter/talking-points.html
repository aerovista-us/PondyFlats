Design 4 talking points

Positioning: protect four practical enclosed stalls in two full 22×22 detached garages, then solve the homes and access around them.

Customer-readable facts

  • Garage B is north and belongs to Home B; Garage A is south and belongs to Home A.
  • Pennsylvania is the only modeled vehicle access.
  • Both garages are detached accessory structures with no living space.
  • Home B is about 1,784 SF gross diagram shell; Home A about 1,759 SF.
  • Each planning diagram has three bedrooms and a connectivity-gated circulation path.

Do not overclaim

Do not call the concept zoning-approved, code-approved, permit-ready, structurally validated, civil-certified, or construction-ready. Use Workbench audit results as design-development geometry evidence only.
