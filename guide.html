What we’re doing on Lot 2

This study asks whether two ~1,800 SF homes with credible parking can fit Pondy Flats Lot 2 without lying about circulation. Geometry comes first; pretty pictures are not allowed to invent a different building. Twin 22×22 programs failed — the active gate is the Parking Reset.

What this study is

Lot 2 is a narrow, irregular parcel. Early “nice duplex” sketches kept failing once a real SUV had to enter from Pennsylvania, park in enclosed garages, and leave again. The work shifted from concept shopping to a proof chain:

  • Freeze the lot — survey polygon and drawing convention never change.
  • Prove access — J1’s locked circulation was called Access A (FS-SUV from Pennsylvania).
  • Accept the trade — parking owns the ground plane; living uses remaining ground pods plus living-over-garage / upper area.
  • Design on that skeleton — J1 Pennsylvania Loft Duplex; J1-B was advanced, then stopped at ownership.
  • Anti-drift visuals — each image constrained by the one before it.

Locked drawing convention

Do not rotate the parcel to make north point up. The compass intentionally points left.

  • Pennsylvania Avenue = RIGHT side of the drawing (south / front), 50′ frontage
  • North / rear = LEFT
  • 148′ runs horizontally from rear/left toward Pennsylvania/right
  • Irregular boundary stays along the bottom; 57.01′ at left/rear
  • Drives originate at Pennsylvania and travel west/left into the lot
  • Math coords: +X toward Pennsylvania (right), +Y down toward the irregular edge
